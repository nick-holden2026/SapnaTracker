Safety & wellness log

🚨
PCOS safety reminders
  1. Never change metformin, inositol, spironolactone or combined OCP doses without your prescriber. Take metformin with food.
  2. Spironolactone is potassium-sparing — avoid potassium supplements, and report dizziness, palpitations or unusual muscle weakness.
  3. HIIT raises cortisol. If you feel wired, anxious, sleep-poor or unusually fatigued, drop to moderate-intensity cardio for a week.
  4. Missed period > 3 months, heavy bleeding, or severe pelvic pain → see a gynaecologist or endocrinologist. Do not self-treat with diet alone.
  5. If you take metformin and train fasted, watch for hypoglycaemia — dizziness, shakiness, sweating, confusion. Stop and eat fast carbs.

Before every gym session

Stop training if…

  • Dizziness or feeling faint
  • Unusual weakness or shakiness
  • Nausea or vomiting
  • Chest pain or palpitations
  • Severe breathlessness
  • Confusion or difficulty speaking
📞
If symptoms are severe or do not resolve, seek emergency care immediately.

Cross-device sync

Optional. Sync your data between phone and PC using a free Firebase Realtime Database. Same config + room code on both devices = automatic two-way sync.

Status: Not configured

📖 How to set up Firebase (2 minutes)
  1. Go to console.firebase.google.com and create a free project.
  2. In the sidebar, click Build → Realtime Database → Create Database. Choose a region, then start in test mode.
  3. Click the ⚙️ gear → Project settings → scroll to Your apps → click the </> web icon.
  4. Register the app (any nickname), then copy the firebaseConfig object it shows you.
  5. Paste it into the box below, then generate a room code.
  6. On your other device, paste the same config + room code.

⚠️ The room code is your only secret — anyone who has it can read/write your data. Use the Generate button to make a long random one.
